Energy Therapy/Spiritual Coaching

Leah is an Energetic and Emotional Wellness Practitioner, with a passion for helping people find balance and healing by working with their energy and emotions. Together, she will help you work towards being a more balanced, vibrant, and authentic you!

Megan

Curated Massage Rituals · Massage

Megan is a Licensed Massage Therapist from Stroudsburg, PA, and a graduate of the Massage Academy of the Poconos. Before becoming an LMT, she worked as a Certified Nursing Assistant, where her passion for caring for others first began. Megan specializes in therapeutic, results-driven massage tailored to each guest's needs. Her techniques include cupping, Graston® Technique, hot stones, assisted stretching, myofascial release, and trigger point therapy to help relieve pain, reduce tension, improve mobility, and promote relaxation. Her goal is simple: to help every guest leave feeling better than when they arrived. Megan is passionate about supporting wellness through compassionate, personalized care and is excited to meet and care for the Fawn Beauty community.

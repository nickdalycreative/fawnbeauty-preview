Owner & Lead Artist

Fawn Monique

Fawn Monique loves helping others feel beautiful both inside and out. She uses her expertise in skin treatments, energy work, and makeup artistry to help her guests feel their best. With a passion for creating beauty, Fawn has dedicated her life to making people feel special.

A certified energy healing practitioner, she combines ancient healing practices with modern day energy work. Fawn is also a veteran make-up artist, educator, and fixture at New York City and London Fashion Weeks working backstage to create the trends for fashion houses like Naeem Khan, Oscar De La Renta, Sachin & Babi, Nick Graham, Michael Kors and Nautica.

Known for her talent, vision, professionalism, and technical expertise, she has over 20 years in the industry working as a make-up artist for beauty editors and production companies from New York to L.A.

Cancellation policy

If a guest fails to show up for their scheduled appointment without providing a 24-hour notice of cancellation, we kindly request that payment for the services be made in advance for any future appointments. This policy helps us ensure that valuable time is not wasted and that our therapists can continue providing quality services to all our guests.

Diversity at Fawn Beauty

My goal is to make every
being feel most beautiful.

As a makeup artist, I have always striven to be inclusive in my work. I believe every person has unique beauty, and I work to bring out that beauty. I create looks that transcend ethnicity, culture, ability, and age.

Whether working with a celebrity, a bride-to-be, or a person just looking for a little self-expression, I am passionate about making sure the techniques and products I use are appropriate for each unique individual guest. I understand that makeup is a reflection of one’s own personal identity.

I am personally very passionate about making sure that all beings, no matter their complexion, look and feel beautiful and confident. I strive to make sure that every guest I work with knows that they are not only appreciated, but also seen and celebrated.

At our studio we strive to create a welcoming and inclusive environment for everyone. We emphasize respect for all genders, ages, abilities, cultures and backgrounds, and our team is dedicated to providing a safe and supportive space for all guests.

Winds of Life

End-of-life & bedside ritual

During the end of life or post-traumatic experiences, loved ones often experience intense emotions that can be difficult to process. An in-home or hospice bedside ritual can be a comforting way to help loved ones cope with grief and feelings of loss, and provide physical comfort using touch therapy.

Fawn, our doula and energy therapist, creates a beautiful atmosphere of peace for your loved one, helping them ease through the physical and emotional waves of their challenges. Each session is customized to your loved ones’ desires.
